The short version

I have spent my career inside young companies, building the first working version of things that did not exist yet.

That path runs from IoT hardware, through language models and a recruitment automation product, to the logistics platform I now lead as CTO. Along the way I started a fintech venture of my own, which taught me to think about a product from the balance sheet backward as well as from the code forward.

What ties it together is a habit. I start from what the customer and the operation actually need, make the technical call that serves it, and keep the result reliable enough to run a business on.
